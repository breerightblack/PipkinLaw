Next steps

What to do after a truck crash

  1. Get medical attention. Even if you feel fine. Adrenaline can mask symptoms for hours or even days.
  2. Call the police and ask for the accident report. Be honest about what happened, but don't admit fault before a full investigation.
  3. Collect contact information. Get names, phone numbers and addresses for the drivers involved and any witnesses.
  4. Document the scene if it's safe. Take photos of the vehicles and your injuries, and note what witnesses say.
  5. Don't talk to the other side's insurer. The adjuster is not on your side. Calls are recorded, and your own words can be used against you. Speak with an attorney first.

Truck accident questions

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and advance the litigation costs. You pay nothing unless we win, and your consultation is free.

How long do I have to file a claim in Texas?

In Texas, injured victims generally have two years from the date of the accident to bring a personal injury lawsuit. Talk to an attorney as early as you can.

Should I talk to the trucking company's insurance adjuster?

Not before you speak with an attorney. Adjusters often use tactics to get you to admit fault or to devalue or deny your claim.
