Next steps

What to do after a car accident

  1. Get medical attention. Even if you feel fine. Adrenaline can mask symptoms for hours or even days.
  2. Collect contact information. Get names, phone numbers and addresses for the drivers involved and any witnesses.
  3. Call the police and ask for the accident report. Be honest about what happened, but don't admit fault before a full investigation.
  4. Document the scene if it's safe. Take photos of both vehicles and your injuries, and note what witnesses say.
  5. Don't talk to the other driver's insurer. The adjuster is not on your side. Calls are recorded, and your own words can be used against you. Speak with an attorney first.

Car accident questions

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in Texas?

In Texas, injured victims generally have two years from the date of the accident to bring a personal injury lawsuit against the wrongdoer.

What if the other driver doesn't have enough insurance?

We look at whether your vehicle provided adequate crash protection. If a defect caused or worsened your injuries, you may have a claim against the manufacturer.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and advance the litigation costs. You pay nothing unless we win, and your consultation is free.
