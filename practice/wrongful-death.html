Justice for the life you lost

A wrongful death lawsuit lets a family seek compensation from those responsible for a loved one's untimely death. It addresses the immediate and long-term financial strain, like the income a breadwinner would have earned over a full life, and it recognizes the pain and suffering of the family left behind.

Big corporations and insurance companies often try to discredit your suffering by shifting blame. For over two decades, we've helped families hold wrongdoers accountable, and doing so can help spare other families from the same loss.

What damages can be recovered?

Economic damages

  • Lost earning capacityThe income your loved one would likely have earned.
  • Lost inheritanceWhat they would likely have saved and left to family.
  • Lost care, support and counselThe value of their contributions to the household.
  • Medical expensesCosts of the final injury or illness.
  • Funeral and burial expenses

Non-economic damages

  • Lost companionship and societyThe love, comfort and companionship your family has lost.
  • Mental anguishThe emotional pain and suffering caused by the death.

Wrongful death questions

What is a wrongful death lawsuit?

In Texas, it's a civil case about compensation, not punishment. It's separate from any criminal case, and you can file one even if the person who caused the death didn't break any laws.

What kinds of accidents lead to wrongful death claims?

Truck accidents, plant explosions, workplace accidents, negligent security, defective products, medical malpractice, car accidents and defective tires, among others. How the death happened determines the type of claim and the proof needed.
