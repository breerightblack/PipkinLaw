Automakers have to build safe vehicles

Manufacturers are responsible for vehicles that are well designed, properly manufactured and safe. Some put profits ahead of public safety, with devastating results. If a defect caused your injuries, or made them worse, you have the right to bring a product liability claim against the manufacturer.

With us on your side, you won't be bullied by large corporate manufacturers and their teams of lawyers.

Why it matters

Why defect cases are different

These cases are notoriously difficult to prove. They take trained experts in engineering, biomechanics and other fields.

  • 1

    Hidden defects

    Many defects aren't apparent at the time of the crash, such as whether a seatbelt engaged properly. Trained attorneys can identify them.

  • 2

    We inspect the vehicle

    We determine whether a defect caused or contributed to your crash, so the manufacturer doesn't escape liability.

  • 3

    Rollover crashes

    We ask three questions: was the roof strong enough, did the seatbelts restrain the occupants, and did the airbags provide adequate protection?

Compensation

What you may be able to recover

If a defective vehicle or part led to your crash, you may be able to hold the manufacturer accountable under strict liability laws.

  • Emergency medical treatment
  • Ongoing and future medical care
  • Lost wages and lost or reduced earning ability
  • Pain and suffering, including emotional trauma
  • Disability and disfigurement
  • Property damage

If a defective vehicle caused the death of a loved one, families may also recover burial and funeral costs, loss of companionship and support, and more.

Questions

Auto defect questions

How do I know if my vehicle was defective?

Under product liability law, a vehicle is defective when it's unreasonably dangerous for its intended purpose. You usually can't tell on your own. Our attorneys inspect the vehicle to find out.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and advance the litigation costs. You pay nothing unless we win, and your consultation is free.
