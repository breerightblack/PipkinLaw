Case results

Record-setting verdicts and settlements for clients statewide.

Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome. Each case is different. The settlements and verdicts listed here are representative of cases handled by The Pipkin Law Firm.

  • $17.5M Gross negligence & wrongful death

    A jury found the company rented our client the wrong equipment and failed to train him to operate it safely, leading to his paralysis. He died weeks later, leaving a wife and child.

  • $12M 18-wheeler & wrongful death

    An 18-wheeler driver looking for his cell phone rear-ended our clients' car as they waited to turn into their church parking lot, killing two women inside.

  • $11M Commercial vehicle & wrongful death

    Recovered for the family of a man killed on his way to work when a commercial vehicle crossed the center line. He was survived by his wife and four children.

  • $6.5M 18-wheeler & wrongful death

    Recovered on behalf of our clients following the death of L. Johnson, caused by the negligence of an 18-wheeler truck driver.

  • $6.5M 18-wheeler & spinal injuries

    Recovered for P. Villereal after an 18-wheeler driver carelessly collided with his vehicle, leaving him with lifelong injuries.

  • $5M+ Negligent auto repair & wrongful death

    A police officer was struck on the highway by a car that hydroplaned on an improperly installed tire. The tire shop was held responsible along with the driver.
