Frequently asked questions

What does a Dallas personal injury attorney do?

A personal injury attorney represents people who have been injured by someone else's negligence or wrongful actions. That means evaluating your right to compensation, investigating what happened (gathering evidence, interviewing witnesses and consulting experts), handling all communication with the defendants and their insurers, negotiating a fair settlement, filing a lawsuit when needed, and taking your case to trial if the wrongdoers won't pay fair compensation.

How do I choose a personal injury attorney in Dallas?

Start with your needs. Like doctors, injury lawyers have different practice areas; we're known across the Metroplex for truck accidents, plant explosions, auto and tire defects, and catastrophic injuries. Then research past results, peer reviews and awards, ask friends and family for referrals, and make sure the firm has the resources to take your case to trial, since most injury firms front the costs of litigation. Finally, meet the attorney and see if they're a good fit.

How can a personal injury attorney help me?

An attorney helps you receive fair and just compensation by holding wrongdoers responsible. The law entitles you to compensation for injuries caused by someone else's negligence, but proving it is complex, especially when trained lawyers are working to help the wrongdoer escape liability and minimize your injuries.

How much does a personal injury attorney cost?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee: at the end of your case, our fee is a percentage of your total recovery. We advance the litigation costs, and you pay nothing unless we win. Your consultation is free.
