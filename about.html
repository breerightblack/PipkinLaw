About the firm

I used to defend insurance companies. Now I fight them.

Steve Pipkin spent the start of his career winning cases for insurers and corporations. Since 2008, he's used that experience for injured people and their families.

Our firm

Aggressive, personal and compassionate

The Pipkin Law Firm represents people across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex who have suffered serious injury because of someone else's negligence or wrongdoing, from car and trucking accidents to premises liability, accidents at work and wrongful death.

If you or a loved one has been hurt, the most important decision you can make toward recovery is choosing a firm with the experience, reputation and track record to help you through it. There's no reason to go through a personal injury alone.

We work on a contingency fee. You pay nothing unless we win.

Smaller firm by design

You're not "just a number"

Steve never wanted a large personal injury firm. Big firms run on a "volume business" attitude. Their clients never know which lawyer is handling their case, and they go weeks without a return phone call.

Here, every client has the attorney's cell phone number. Clients get their calls and texts returned. It's a personal commitment.

Resources of a large firm

Built to handle any case

Like larger firms, we have the resources to handle the most complex cases. We have the financial resources to hire industry experts and other professionals to advise and serve as witnesses, so our clients get the best financial recovery.

Career

  1. 1992

    Chose the courtroom over a big-firm offer

    After law school, Steve turned down a job with Jenkins & Gilchrist, a high-powered civil defense firm where young lawyers rarely went to trial. He took a $30,000 pay cut to work for Allstate Insurance Company and try cases from day one.

  2. Allstate

    Two years, more than 95% of cases won

    Steve built his trial record defending Allstate.

  3. Defense firms

    McCauley, McDonald, Love & Devin, then Stradley & Wright

    He defended doctors, nurses and hospitals in medical malpractice cases, then became an associate and partner at Stradley & Wright, winning 96% of his trials. Clients included Allstate, Whataburger, Dillard's, Flying J and Jack in the Box (Food Maker, Inc.).

  4. Plaintiff's side

    Four years with top plaintiff trial lawyers

    Steve worked with Mike Kaeske at the Kaeske Law Firm and Collen Clark at Schmidt & Clark.

  5. 2008

    Founded The Pipkin Law Firm

    Steve formed his own firm to represent everyday people fighting corporate giants and insurance companies: car wrecks, 18-wheeler crashes, nursing home neglect, police excessive force and premises injuries, including assaults at apartment complexes, hotels and other public places.
